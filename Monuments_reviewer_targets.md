# Monuments reviewer and discovery targets

These are research targets, not guaranteed placements. Review each outlet's current policy before sending anything. Offer a free copy for honest consideration; never imply that a positive review is expected.

| Target | Link | Why it fits | Outreach route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ginger Nuts of Horror | https://gnofhorror.com/ | Established horror publication with in-depth book reviews and cosmic-horror coverage. | Use the site's current contact or review-request route; check policy first. |
| Hellnotes | https://hellnotes.com/contact-us/ | Horror-focused publication that provides a review-request contact form. | Submit the requested title, author, publisher, ISBN/list price, and word/page count. |
| Erica Robyn Reads | https://www.ericarobynreads.com/about-me/request-review/ | Horror book blogger with a public review-request process. | Read acceptance rules and submit through the listed form. |

| Reedsy horror review-blog directory | https://reedsy.com/resources/book-review-blogs/horror/ | Useful current directory for finding additional active horror blogs. | Use it for discovery; contact each reviewer individually rather than copying one message. |

## Personalization notes

Before contacting an outlet, find one recent review or article and mention the specific overlap with *Monuments*: cosmic horror, supernatural investigation, unreliable memory, impossible architecture, morally compromised narrators, or literary noir.

Do not claim that *Monuments* is similar to a writer or book you have not actually read. Do not send attachments in the first message unless the outlet requests them. Link to the first chapter first, then send the EPUB only after interest.
