## Posting rules

- Use the same public identity: M. Fawkes.
- Include the website only when it serves the post.
- Reply to comments before making the next direct promotion.
- Do not use fake reader testimonials or invented review quotes.
- Do not imply that an ARC requires a favorable review.
- Repost the best-performing concept later with a new image or wording rather than repeating it immediately.
