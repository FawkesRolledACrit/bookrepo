The work

M. Fawkes writes stories about people who build explanations to survive things that cannot be explained. The work sits at the intersection of literary horror, weird fiction, and hard-boiled investigation, with a particular interest in corrupted memory, impossible architecture, and the moral cost of looking away.

Monuments is the debut novella: a supernatural noir investigation that begins with a dead student, an impossible message, and a town that will not remain in one shape. It asks what a person owes the suffering they helped create—and whether shutting a machine down can ever be called mercy.
