## Posting cadence

Use a light, sustainable rhythm:

- 3 atmosphere or reader-interest posts for every direct promotional post
- 2–4 posts per week before release
- 1 direct ARC/release post per platform per campaign period
- Reply to genuine responses before starting another promotion

Do not copy-paste the same message into multiple communities. Adapt each post to the conversation and disclose that M. Fawkes is the author.

## ARC handling checklist

- Verify that the reviewer actually covers horror, weird fiction, or adjacent genres.
- Personalize the first paragraph with a real reference to their work.
- Offer EPUB only after they express interest.
- Keep a record of the date contacted and response status.
- Never ask for a favorable review.
- Never send repeated follow-ups after a clear decline or no response.
- Ask permission before adding anyone to an ongoing mailing list.
