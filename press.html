Short description

Monuments

A private investigator follows a dead student's last message into a drowned New England town where the buildings rearrange themselves, a theater casts him as its patient, and the machinery recognizes his hands.

Genre: literary cosmic horror, supernatural noir, weird fiction

Status: final review; release date forthcoming

Author bio

Short bio: M. Fawkes writes cosmic horror, supernatural noir, and strange fiction about memory, identity, culpability, and what people build to survive the impossible.

Long bio: M. Fawkes writes stories about people who build explanations to survive things that cannot be explained. Their work combines literary horror, weird fiction, and hard-boiled investigation, with a particular interest in corrupted memory, impossible architecture, and morally compromised narrators. Monuments is their debut novella.
