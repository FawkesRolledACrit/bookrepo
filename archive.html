Notice to the reader

This file was assembled from public records, private correspondence, and material recovered near the former Monserta Glassworks. Several dates conflict. The archive has declined to correct them.

Document A / Local paper

The Serwick Sentinel

October 13, 2019

BODY FOUND IN ABANDONED MONSERTA GLASSWORKS FACTORY

Police say no foul play. Victim likely died of heart attack. The property manager expressed his “great disheartenment” and again advised trespassers to seek their thrills elsewhere.

The article ends: “We will continue to update our readers as the story develops.” No subsequent editions have been located.

Document B / Visitor information

Visit Marvelous Serwick!

Come stay at the luxurious four-star Marlin Inn. Enjoy an all-expenses-paid deep sea fishing trip, private beaches, and historical landmarks like the ancient Church of Eternal Resplendence.

The brochure is dated 1987. The hotel photograph appears to have been taken from inside the building.
